Getting around

Walking

The centre and Old Town are easy to walk. Most of the places on this page are 10 to 20 minutes from the hotel. Street signs say iela, which means street.

Bolt

The quickest way to get a car or a scooter. Download the app before you arrive.

Buses, trams and trolleybuses

Buy a 90-minute ticket in the Mobilly app before you get on. It costs €1.50 and covers transfers. Most vehicles don’t sell tickets on board.

Good to know

Tap water
Riga’s tap water is safe to drink.
Cash
You probably won’t need cash. Cards and contactless payments are widely accepted, and the local currency is the euro (€).
Tipping
Around 10% for good restaurant service is appreciated, but not mandatory.
English
English is widely spoken in cafés, restaurants, hotels, and most places you’ll visit.
Latvians
We can be a little reserved with strangers, but don’t mistake that for being unfriendly. We’re just not big on small talk!
Power
Latvia uses Type C/F sockets with 230V power, so you’ll need a European plug adapter. Keep in mind that an adapter doesn’t convert voltage, so double-check your equipment before plugging it in.
Time difference
Riga is usually 7 hours ahead of New York and 10 hours ahead of Los Angeles.
Temperature
We use Celsius instead of Fahrenheit. For reference, 5°C is 41°F.
Restroom
If you’re looking for a restroom, look for signs saying “WC.” That’s what we call it here.
