# NECom website — full audit (Phase 1)

Reviewed 6 Nov 2026 against `main` @ `b3e4436` and the live site, https://www.necom.lv.
Scope: code, build health, the live site at 360/390/430/1440 px, a11y, performance, SEO, security.
Copy is out of scope. I only flag content that is objectively wrong, inconsistent, or out of date.

---

## Executive summary

The site's overall look holds up: a dark hero, generous white space, a restrained palette and real event photography. It does not need a redesign.
Underneath, it is an unmaintained v0.dev export. `generator: 'v0.app'` is still in the `<head>`, about 45 shadcn components were never used, two `globals.css` files exist, deps are pinned to `"latest"`, and **Next.js 15.3.8 has unpatched critical advisories**.
Three things quietly undercut the "Apple-level polish" goal:
1. **Inter is downloaded but never applied.** Every visitor sees their OS system font.
2. **The colour tokens are broken.** Every "muted" grey paragraph renders at full near-black body colour.
3. **The cookie banner does nothing.** Analytics load whether or not you click Accept.

SEO basics exist but are mis-wired. No JSON-LD is in the server HTML. The About page is `noindex`. The sitemap lists a redirect.
Live dates are going stale. The "2026 Calendar" and the footer's "Upcoming" list already show past events. Everything is hard-coded for 2026.

**Biggest wins, roughly a day each:**
- Upgrade Next.js and React, and remove the junk dependencies.
- Fix the font and colour tokens. This is the single most visible polish gain.
- Make the date-driven data self-expiring.
- Fix the JSON-LD, robots and sitemap wiring.
- Move the 12.7 MB hero video and all images off `rimirigamarathon.com`.
- Add reduced-motion support and focus states.

## Feels AI-ish / generic

Blunt version: anyone who has used v0 will spot it within seconds in the code, and in about four places on the page.

1. **Emoji as icons.** `components/sections/by-the-numbers-section.tsx` uses ❤️ 🌳 🐄 next to the €150,000, 10 hectares and 26 cows stats, plus a two-row marquee of **emoji flags**, tilted `-rotate-6 scale-125`, for "116 countries". Emoji render differently on every OS (Windows shows letter pairs instead of flags) and are the clearest AI tell on the site.
   *Instead:* typographic stats only (number plus label) on a quiet grid. For the countries, show the number large; if you want texture, a monochrome dotted world map or a plain wrapping list of ISO codes in tabular figures.
2. **The bento grid of identical rounded cards** (same file). Eight `rounded-2xl` tiles, each a different colour or style: a red card, a grey card, a black "Seiko" board with fake seven-segment digits, a game. This is the exact "dashboard bento" v0 produces for any "stats section" prompt. The `/events` hero (`app/events/page.tsx:39-84`) repeats it: three white `rounded-2xl` boxes inside a `rounded-2xl bg-neutral-100` box.
   *Instead:* a Swiss stat row. Four to six columns of big numbers with hairline dividers and small captions, all on white, with one accent colour at most. Keep photography as full-bleed images, not as tiles with badges.
3. **The bottle-sabrage mini-game and the Seiko clock** (`by-the-numbers-section.tsx`, `world-athletics-riga23/page.tsx`). Clever, but they are effects for their own sake and break the restrained tone. They're also the least accessible elements on the site.
   *Instead:* cut both, or keep exactly one as a deliberate easter egg on its own event page and build it properly (keyboard support, reduced motion). The world records read better as a plain three-row table with tabular numerals.
4. **The shadcn default palette left unchanged.** `--foreground: 222.2 84% 4.9%` and the bluish slate `--muted` in `app/globals.css:6-40` are the stock `npx shadcn init` values, plus `sidebar-*` and `chart-*` tokens for components that don't exist.
   *Instead:* see P1-04, a neutral grey ramp of your own.
5. **Pill-on-photo badges and glass circles everywhere.** `bg-white/20 backdrop-blur-sm rounded-full` badges with an `ArrowUpRight` in a frosted circle (`by-the-numbers-section.tsx`, `baltic-brand-forum/page.tsx:39`, `event-header.tsx:25`), and the decorative ring "pattern" in `event-countdown.tsx:57-60` (two blurred circles, which is the gradient-blob move).
   *Instead:* plain uppercase small caps in white, no container. Remove the decorative circles.
6. **Stock lucide icons in grey squares.** `components/event-detail/event-stats.tsx` puts `Award` and `Users` in `bg-gray-200 rounded-xl` chips next to "First Held In" and "Annual Participants". The 404 has a house icon in its button; `error.tsx` has a warning triangle in a red circle.
   *Instead:* label and value, no icon. Keep icons only where they carry meaning (external link ↗, play/pause).
7. **Hover scale and lift on everything.** `hover:scale-105` (email CTA, social buttons, event images), `hover:scale-[1.02]` (countdown), `hover:shadow-lg` (buttons), `group-hover:translate-x-1` arrows, and staggered `fadeSlideIn` on mega-menu cards (`navigation.tsx:196-200`).
   *Instead:* one hover language, such as an opacity or underline change for links and a background shift for buttons. Images can stay static or use a very slow 1.02 on photos only.
8. **Copy-pasted section skeletons.** About, Journal, Our Events, Our team, Trusted By and the Events page list all use the same `h2` plus muted one-liner plus content shape, with an IntersectionObserver fade-up pasted five times. The home "Our Events" block (`events-showcase-section.tsx`) is a near-duplicate of the `/events` list.
   *Instead:* give the home events block its own, more compact treatment (e.g. a typographic index list with year and type in columns, image on hover or focus) and leave the alternating image/text layout to `/events`.
9. **The "View All" dashed card** in the mega menu (`navigation.tsx:238-258`): a `border-2 border-dashed` tile with an arrow in a circle is the classic template "add new" affordance.
   *Instead:* a plain text link, "All events →", in the menu header.
10. **Code tells.** `generator: 'v0.app'` (`layout.tsx:77`); an ASCII-art HTML comment injected through a `dangerouslySetInnerHTML` `div` (`app/page.tsx:18-35`, which leaves an empty `div` in the DOM); `"/placeholder.svg?width=800&height=450"` fallbacks to a file that's a v0 placeholder; `images/design-mode/` (the v0 "design mode" upload folder) as the main image directory; JSX strings wrapped as `{"…"}` everywhere.
    *Instead:* remove the `generator` tag and placeholders, rename `design-mode/` to `images/events|journal|team`, and move the ASCII comment into `layout.tsx` as a real HTML comment if you want to keep it.

What doesn't feel generic and should stay: the black hero with the large three-word H1, the mix-blend logo, the large real event photography, and the floating pill nav on desktop. These are the brand. Polish them; don't replace them.
